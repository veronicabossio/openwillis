The local bakery, which recently reopened after renovations, has been attracting more customers than ever. People line up early in the morning to buy freshly baked sourdough and croissants. According to the owner, the new layout makes the baking process more efficient, and the addition of outdoor seating has helped business thrive. One customer mentioned that the environment now feels both more spacious and welcoming, especially on sunny days. Children often stop by after school to grab cookies, while adults enjoy espresso and conversation in the shaded courtyard
